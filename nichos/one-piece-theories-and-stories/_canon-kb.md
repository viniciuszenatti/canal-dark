>
> ## How to read this file (truth rules)
> - **Truth hierarchy:** One Piece Wiki (Fandom) **>** SBS (Oda's Q&A) **>** Vivre Card databook **>** labeled speculation.
> - Every line carries a **source anchor** (chapter / SBS volume / databook) AND a tag:
>   - **[FACT]** = confirmed in the manga / SBS / databook (canon).
>   - **[THEORY]** = fan speculation. NEVER present as "Oda confirmed/revealed."
>   - **[UNVERIFIED]** = referenced widely but I could not independently confirm the exact anchor — treat as soft until checked in the manga/wiki by hand.
> - **Time-anchor everything**: lore is "as of ~Ch. 1140 (mid-2026)". ~4 chapters/month can move things. Prefer evergreen when scripting.
> - **VIZ official EN spelling** is used throughout: "Joy Boy", "Im" (the wiki romanizes the in-panel name as **Nerona Imu**; VIZ uses "Im" — both noted), "Poneglyph", "Yonko", "Laugh Tale", "Haki", "Devil Fruit".

Items I could only see second-hand are tagged [UNVERIFIED]. **No lore or source was invented.** When in doubt, the human reviewer should open the cited wiki page / chapter in a browser.

## 1. NAMES & TERMS (VIZ spelling)

### 1.1 People — Straw Hats & allies
- **Monkey D. Luffy** — protagonist; ate the fruit now revealed as the Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika (formerly called Gomu Gomu no Mi). Carries the Will of the D. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1044*.
- **Roronoa Zoro** — swordsman; confirmed a descendant of the **Shimotsuki** clan of Wano, tied by blood/lineage to the legendary samurai **Shimotsuki Ryuma**. **[FACT]** — *SBS Vol. 105* (Shimotsuki ancestry). Ryuma himself originates in Oda's one-shots *Monsters* / *Great Swordsmen* and the swordsman prototype for Zoro. **[FACT]** — *Monsters (one-shot)*.
- **Nico Robin** — sole survivor of Ohara; the only living person who can read Poneglyphs. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 395 area / Enies Lobby*. Reunited with Saul at Elbaf and saw the saved Ohara books. **[FACT]** — *Elbaf arc, 2025 chapters (~Ch. 1130s)*.

### 1.2 People — Roger Pirates / the past
- **Gol D. Roger** — the Pirate King; first (with his crew) to reach the final island and learn the true history; named the island "Laugh Tale." Carries the Will of the D. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 967–968*.
- **Joy Boy** — central Void Century figure; the "first pirate"/leader tied to the Great Kingdom who left the treasure and the Poneglyph apology to Fish-Man Island. Original wielder of the Nika power. **[FACT that he existed and left messages]** — *Ch. 967–968, 1043–1044, 1114–1115*. (His exact identity/goals = open mystery, see §4.)
- **Shimotsuki Ryuma** — "Sword God" of Wano ~400 years ago; Zoro's legendary ancestor line. **[FACT]** — *Wano arc / SBS Vol. 105*.

### 1.3 People — the World Government's apex
- **Im** (wiki romanization **Nerona Imu**) — secret true ruler of the World Government; occupies the **Empty Throne** in Pangaea Castle, Mariejois. One of the original **First Twenty** founders and the only one still alive after 800 years. **[FACT that Im exists, sits the Empty Throne, and is a founder]** — *Ch. 906–908 (silhouette/throne); Ch. 1086 (name "Nerona Imu" + founder status)*. (Im's powers/immortality mechanism = open mystery, see §4.)
- **The Five Elders / Gorosei** — the five highest WG authorities under Im, each with a "Warrior God" (Saint) title revealed in Ch. 1086: **Jaygarcia Saturn** (Scientific Defense), **Marcus Mars** (the Environment), **Topman Warcury** (Justice/Legal Affairs), **Ethanbaron V. Nusjuro** (Finance), **Shepherd Ju Peter** (Agriculture). **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1086*. (Exact title-to-name spellings vary slightly across fan translations; **[UNVERIFIED]** on a couple of romanizations — verify in VIZ.)
- **Figarland Garling** — leader of the **Holy Knights / Knights of God**; head of the Figarland family; was the "God's Knight" figure tied to God Valley. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1086 / 1095–1096 area*.
- **Figarland Shamrock** — World Noble, commander of the Holy Knights, and the **older twin brother of Shanks** (sons of Garling). Introduced in the Elbaf arc. **[FACT]** — *Elbaf arc, 2025 (~Ch. 1130s)*. (Confirms the long-running "Shanks twin" theory.)

### 1.4 People — the Yonko (current, post-Wano)
- **Shanks (Red-Haired)** — Emperor; the only current Yonko who was also in the prior lineup. Bounty ~4.048B. **[FACT]** — *post-Wano databook/chapters*.
- **Marshall D. Teach (Blackbeard)** — Emperor; rose after defeating Whitebeard; carries the Will of the D.; has a uniquely abnormal body able to hold multiple Devil Fruit powers. **[FACT that his body is abnormal/holds 2+ fruits]** — *Marineford / Ch. 1059*. The popular "Cerberus fruit / three souls" explanation was **DEBUNKED** by the Shamrock Cerberus-sword reveal. **[FACT of debunk]** — *Ch. 1138 (2025)*. The true mechanism is still **[THEORY]**.
- **Monkey D. Luffy** — became an Emperor after Onigashima/Wano. **[FACT]** — *post-Wano*.
- **Buggy the Clown** — became an Emperor essentially by reputation (figurehead of Cross Guild; "subduing" Mihawk & Crocodile). Notable: the only current/former Emperor with no Haki. **[FACT]** — *post-Wano / Cross Guild reveal*.

### 1.5 People — Marines / Revolutionaries
- **Monkey D. Garp** — Marine hero ("Garp the Fist"); Luffy's grandfather; carries the Will of the D. **[FACT]** — *Marineford era*.
- **Monkey D. Dragon** — leader of the **Revolutionary Army**, Luffy's father, world's most wanted criminal; carries the Will of the D. **[FACT]** — *Romance Dawn / Ch. 100s; revealed as father Ch. 432*.
- **Sabo** — Revolutionary Army Chief of Staff; ate the Mera Mera no Mi (Ace's old flame fruit). **[FACT]** — *Dressrosa; alive-confirmation Ch. 1082*.
- **Jaguar D. Saul** — former giant Marine vice admiral who saved young Robin at Ohara; survived and led giants to preserve the Ohara books at Elbaf. Carries the Will of the D. **[FACT]** — *Ohara flashback; survival/Elbaf reveal 2025 (~Ch. 1066 hint, confirmed in Elbaf arc)*.

### 1.6 Factions & organizations
- **World Government (WG)** — global authority formed when the "Twenty Kingdoms" defeated the Great Kingdom ~800 years ago and renamed themselves. Secretly ruled by Im. **[FACT]** — *Void Century lore, Ch. 1086 / 1114–1115*.
- **Marines** — the WG's military/naval force. **[FACT]** — *series-wide*.
- **Yonko (Four Emperors)** — the four most powerful pirate captains, a balance-of-power pillar alongside the Marines and the (now-abolished) Warlords. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 432-era introduction*.
- **Cross Guild** — New World crime syndicate fronted by Buggy but truly run by **Dracule Mihawk** and **Sir Crocodile**; formed after the Warlord (Shichibukai) system was abolished at the Reverie. **[FACT]** — *introduced Ch. 1056*.
- **Revolutionary Army** — anti-WG organization led by Dragon, HQ on Momoiro Island; aims to overthrow the World Nobles/WG. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1058 (HQ shown)*.
- **Holy Knights / Knights of God** — elite force serving directly under Im, led by Figarland Garling; members hold a "Deep Sea Covenant" with Im granting abnormal powers. **[FACT that they exist & serve Im, led by Garling]** — *Ch. 1086 / Elbaf arc*. Exact covenant mechanics partly **[UNVERIFIED]** (fan-translation detail) — verify in VIZ.
- **The First Twenty / Twenty Kingdoms** — the 20 royal families who founded the WG; 19 descend into the **World Nobles (Celestial Dragons / Tenryūbito)** who relocated to Mariejois; the Donquixote family famously left. Im was among the original founders. **[FACT]** — *Reverie arc / Ch. 1086*.

### 1.7 Places
- **Mariejois (Mary Geoise)** — the WG's holy capital atop the Red Line; home of the World Nobles, **Pangaea Castle**, the **Empty Throne**, and a hidden **National Treasure**. **[FACT]** — *Reverie arc, Ch. 906–908*.
- **Laugh Tale** — the final island at the end of the Grand Line where Roger's crew learned the true history; named by Roger because the truth was a "funny tale." Holds the treasure called **One Piece**. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 967–968*.
- **The Reverie (Levely)** — the quadrennial world council of WG-member kings at Mariejois; setting for the Im reveal and the Warlord abolition. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 903–908*.
- **Wano Country** — isolated samurai nation; home of the **Kozuki family** (the Poneglyph carvers) and a hidden **Road Poneglyph**; **Pluton** rests in its submerged area. **[FACT]** — *Wano arc, Ch. 909–1057*.
- **Ohara** — island of scholars (led by Prof. Clover) destroyed by Buster Call for researching the Void Century; Robin's home. **[FACT]** — *Ohara flashback*.
- **Elbaf** — homeland of the giants; current arc; location of the preserved Ohara library (the "Owl/Tree library") and a candidate for the missing 4th Road Poneglyph. **[FACT that the Ohara books are there]** — *Elbaf arc 2025*. (Road Poneglyph location = **[THEORY/UNVERIFIED]**.)
- **Fish-Man Island** — underwater kingdom (Ryugu Kingdom); home of Poseidon/Shirahoshi; recipient of Joy Boy's apology Poneglyph. **[FACT]** — *Fish-Man Island arc*.
- **God Valley** — site of the famous incident 38 years ago (Rocks vs Roger+Garp); birthplace of Shanks & Shamrock. **[FACT of incident]** — *Ch. 1095–1096 area*.

### 1.8 Core concepts
- **Poneglyphs** — indestructible stone blocks inscribed in an ancient script, **carved by the Kozuki family** of Wano, preserving Void Century history; only readable by trained scholars (now only Robin). Researching them is the WG's "worst crime." **[FACT]** — *Ch. 395-area; carver reveal Wano*.
- **Road Poneglyphs** — the four red Poneglyphs whose combined coordinates point to Laugh Tale. Confirmed locations: **Zou**, **Whole Cake Island** (Big Mom's), **Wano** (Kozuki's). The **4th** is the open question. **[FACT for the three]** — *Zou/WCI/Wano arcs*. 4th location = **[THEORY]**.
- **Rio Poneglyph** — the single Poneglyph said to tell the complete true history when all are read together; located at Laugh Tale. **[FACT that it exists / is the "true history" stone]** — *Ch. 967-area reveal*. (Exact contents = unknown.)
- **Void Century** — the ~100-year span (≈900–800 years ago) erased from official record; the WG forbids its study. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 395-area; expanded Ch. 1114–1115*.
- **Will of the D.** — the hidden "D." middle initial carried by a lineage (Luffy, Ace, Teach, Law, Dragon, Garp, Saul, Roger…); called by an Elder the "natural enemy of the gods"/tied to "danger"; most carriers don't know its meaning. **[FACT that the lineage & secrecy exist]** — *Ch. 233-area; "enemy of God" Ch. 1085-area*. Meaning = open mystery, see §4.
- **Haki** — willpower-based combat energy in **three confirmed types**: **Observation Haki** (Kenbunshoku), **Armament Haki** (Busoshoku), and **Conqueror's / Supreme King Haki** (Haoshoku, ~1 in a million, can't be trained into existence). **[FACT]** — *Ch. 597-area naming; SBS Vol. 71 details which colors Luffy/Zoro/Sanji favor*.
- **Devil Fruits** — fruits granting powers at the cost of swimming ability; three classes: **Paramecia**, **Zoan**, **Logia**. **[FACT]** — *series-wide; SBS classification*.
- **Devil Fruit Awakening** — an advanced state reached when the user's mind "catches up" to the fruit: Paramecia awakenings let users impose their power on the surroundings (Doflamingo's strings, Katakuri's mochi); Zoan awakenings boost the body and add traits. **[FACT]** — *Impel Down jailer beasts; Dressrosa (Doflamingo); WCI (Katakuri)*.
- **The Ancient Weapons** — three Void Century superweapons capable of world-ending destruction: **Pluton**, **Poseidon**, **Uranus** (see §4 for status of each). **[FACT that the three exist]** — *Ch. 193-area (Pluton); Fish-Man Island (Poseidon); Ch. 1114–1115 (Uranus named)*.

## 2. MACRO TIMELINE (Void Century → present)

All "X years ago" are relative to the current story (Egghead/Elbaf era, ~2026 reading).

- **~900–800 years ago — The Void Century.** A world war: the **Great Kingdom** (allied with Joy Boy) vs the **Twenty Kingdoms**. The Twenty win, erase the Great Kingdom from history, and rename themselves the **World Government**. The Ancient Weapons are used; sea levels rose ~200m, sinking landmasses (per Vegapunk). **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1114–1115; Ch. 1086*.
- **~800 years ago — Founding of the WG.** The First Twenty relocate to Mariejois (becoming World Nobles); the Donquixote family among the founders; **Im** survives and rules from the Empty Throne. The Kozuki carve the Poneglyphs to preserve the truth. **[FACT]** — *Reverie arc; Ch. 1086*.
- **~400 years ago — "Country of Gold" Wano.** Shimotsuki Ryuma's era; the foundation of Zoro's lineage. **[FACT]** — *Wano arc / SBS Vol. 105*.
- **~38 years ago — The God Valley Incident.** Garp + Roger defeat the **Rocks Pirates** (Rocks D. Xebec); Shanks & Shamrock born around this event. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1095–1096 area*.
- **~26 years ago — Roger reaches Laugh Tale.** The Roger Pirates learn the true history, the meaning of the D., the Ancient Weapons, and Joy Boy's story; Roger laughs and names the island. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 967–968*.
- **~24 years ago — Roger's execution.** Roger surrenders, is executed at Loguetown, and his final words spark the Great Pirate Era. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1 / Ch. 966–968*.
- **~22 years ago — The Ohara Incident.** Buster Call destroys Ohara for Poneglyph research; Robin survives; Saul saves her and later preserves the books at Elbaf. **[FACT]** — *Ohara flashback; Elbaf 2025*.
- **Current era — Final Saga.** Wano freed; new Yonko (Luffy, Buggy); the Reverie's Im reveal; the Lulusia Kingdom erased by **Uranus**; **Vegapunk's worldwide broadcast** reveals fragments of the Void Century (Joy Boy's defeat, the 200m sea-rise, that the Ancient Weapons still exist). Story moves Egghead → Elbaf. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1060 (Lulusia); Ch. 1113–1115 (broadcast); Elbaf arc 2025*.

## 4. OPEN LORE HOOKS (the raw material for theories)

Each hook = the canon floor (what's actually confirmed) + the open question (what's fair-game speculation). This is where original theses come from.

### 4.1 Im / the Empty Throne
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** Im exists, is named (Nerona Imu), sits the Empty Throne, is one of the First Twenty, and has survived ~800 years. Im erased Lulusia with Uranus. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 906–908, 1060, 1086*.
- **Open question [THEORY]:** How is Im immortal? What does Im want? Whose Devil Fruit/power keeps Im alive? Is Im connected to the Great Kingdom's enemies specifically? **All speculation — label as such.**

### 4.2 Joy Boy
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** Joy Boy was a real Void Century figure tied to the Great Kingdom, left the treasure and the apology Poneglyph to Fish-Man Island, and his "return" was declared by Zunesha when Luffy awakened. The Nika power is his. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 967–968, 1043–1044, 1114–1115*.
- **Open question [THEORY]:** Was Joy Boy the first Nika user? Was he the original owner of "One Piece"? What promise did he break, and who is meant to fulfill it (Luffy implied)? **Speculation.**

### 4.3 The Will of the D.
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** A hidden lineage carries the "D."; it's been called the "natural enemy of the gods"; carriers usually smile at death; most don't know its meaning. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 233-area; Ch. 1085-area*.
- **Open question [THEORY]:** What does "D." literally stand for (Devil / Dawn / Dreams / Danger are fan reads)? Is it a surviving bloodline of the Great Kingdom? **Speculation — the "danger/enemy of God" flavor is canon, the exact word is not.**

### 4.4 The three Ancient Weapons
- **Pluton — [FACT]:** ancient battleship, rests beneath Wano; blueprints existed (Water 7/Franky lore). *Wano arc; Ch. 193-area.*
- **Poseidon — [FACT]:** a living weapon = the current generation's mermaid princess **Shirahoshi**, able to command Sea Kings. *Fish-Man Island arc.*
- **Uranus — [FACT that it's WG-held & wiped out Lulusia]:** named in Vegapunk's broadcast; powered by the **Mother Flame**. *Ch. 1114–1115.* (Its exact form/full power = partly open → **[THEORY]** for specifics.)
- **Open question [THEORY]:** Will Pluton be Wano's/Franky's? How does Uranus physically work? Could the weapons be "living" by design? **Speculation.**

### 4.5 One Piece (the treasure) & Laugh Tale
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** One Piece is real, sits at Laugh Tale, was left by Joy Boy, and made Roger's crew laugh; reaching it requires all four Road Poneglyphs. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1, 967–968.*
- **Open question [THEORY]:** What IS it physically (object vs knowledge vs both)? Why did it make Roger laugh? Why was Roger "20 years too early"? **Speculation.**

### 4.6 The Void Century & the Great Kingdom
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** A ~100-year war between the Great Kingdom (+Joy Boy) and the Twenty Kingdoms; the winners became the WG and buried the era; Ancient Weapons were used; seas rose ~200m. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1086, 1114–1115.*
- **Open question [THEORY]:** What was the Great Kingdom's name and "intolerable philosophy"? Were they the "D." people? What exactly did they fight FOR? **Speculation.**

### 4.7 Blackbeard's body
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** Teach has an abnormal body that holds at least two Devil Fruits (Yami Yami + Gura Gura) — physically unprecedented. **[FACT]** — *Ch. 1059.* The "Cerberus fruit / three literal souls" explanation is **DEBUNKED**. **[FACT of debunk]** — *Ch. 1138 (2025).*
- **Open question [THEORY]:** What actually lets him hold multiple fruits? Link to Rocks D. Xebec? **Speculation — but do NOT recycle the dead Cerberus version as if live.**

### 4.8 The Mariejois National Treasure / giant Straw Hat
- **Canon floor [FACT]:** A frozen vault under Pangaea Castle holds a giant Straw Hat identical to Luffy's; the WG's "National Treasure" can supposedly "shake the world." **[FACT that the hat & treasure exist]** — *Ch. 906; Doflamingo's "National Treasure" line.*
- **Open question [THEORY]:** Whose hat is it (Joy Boy implied by the Nika gigantification link)? Is the hat itself the National Treasure? **Speculation.**
